# Risk Assessment

## Executive Summary

An internal security assessment was conducted for the fictional organization Botium Toys to evaluate its current security posture and identify risks that could affect business operations and sensitive information.

The assessment identified several significant security weaknesses, particularly involving access control, data protection, business continuity, authentication, and threat detection.

**Overall Risk Level: High**

---

## Key Risk Areas

| Risk Area | Risk Level | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| Access Control | High | Access to sensitive information is not sufficiently restricted using least privilege and separation of duties. |
| Data Protection | High | Sensitive information is not adequately protected through encryption. |
| Business Continuity | High | The organization lacks adequate backups and a formal disaster recovery plan. |
| Authentication | High | Existing password requirements require improvement, and a centralized password management solution is not implemented. |
| Threat Detection | High | An intrusion detection system is not currently deployed. |
| Legacy Systems | Medium | Legacy systems are manually monitored but require a consistent maintenance schedule. |
| Physical Security | Low | Physical security controls including locks, CCTV, and fire detection systems are implemented. |

---

## Potential Impact

If the identified risks are not addressed, the organization could experience:

- Unauthorized access to sensitive information
- Exposure or theft of customer data
- Loss of critical organizational data
- Extended downtime following a security incident
- Difficulty detecting malicious activity
- Increased risk from compromised credentials
- Compliance and regulatory issues

---

## Existing Security Strengths

The assessment also identified several controls that are currently implemented:

- Firewall protection
- Antivirus software
- Physical locks
- CCTV surveillance
- Fire detection and prevention systems
- Breach notification procedures
- Privacy policies and procedures

These controls provide a foundation for the organization's security program but should be supplemented by additional technical and administrative controls.

---

## Risk Prioritization

The highest-priority risks involve protecting sensitive information and maintaining business operations. Access controls, encryption, backups, disaster recovery, password security, and intrusion detection should therefore receive priority during remediation.

---

## Conclusion

The organization's current security posture presents a high level of risk. Although several preventive and physical security controls are implemented, significant gaps remain in access management, data protection, recovery capabilities, authentication, and threat detection.

Addressing these weaknesses would reduce the likelihood and potential impact of security incidents and strengthen the organization's overall security posture.
